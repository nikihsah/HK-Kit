# FAQ

## Is HK-Kit a character builder?

No. HK-Kit is an LLM-native framework and knowledge pack for AI-assisted character design.

## Can MODE CREATE read the original PDF?

No. The PDF is only for maintainers rebuilding HK-RDB.

## Why should the model ask questions?

Because early testing showed that silent assumptions produce technically plausible but fantasy-wrong characters.

## Why is HK-RDB split into files?

Small focused files are easier for AI models to inspect, validate, and reason about than one huge rules blob.

## Why are the data files empty?

The skeleton does not fabricate rules. HK-RDB must be filled from the source rulebook through a reviewed maintenance process.
